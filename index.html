How to read these numbers

  • Rebuilt weekly. Every count comes from the files NCBI, UCSC, GENCODE and EBI publish, pulled every Sunday. A source that fails keeps last week’s numbers and its own date. Tap a mark for its numbers.
  • GRCh38 coordinates. Positions are on GRCh38.p14, counted from 1 with both ends included, as genome browsers show them. The UCSC tables count from 0; they are converted.
  • Two assemblies. GRCh38 is the reference the databases annotate. T2T-CHM13v2.0 is the first gapless assembly, from one cell line, with chromosome Y from a second person.
  • Chromosomes only. Counts cover chromosomes 1 to 22, X, Y and the mitochondrial genome where it says so. Alternate loci, patches and unplaced scaffolds are counted apart.
  • Log scales. Charts that span several orders of magnitude say log scale in their caption.
  • Every figure’s numbers. Each caption ends with a table and a CSV of exactly what is drawn.
